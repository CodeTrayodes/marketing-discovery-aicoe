Other ideas on the horizon
A
GEO Studio
Test your content against multiple LLMs. See which queries trigger a citation, which sentences are most "citation-friendly," and get targeted rewrites to increase LLM visibility.
B
Brief Suggestion Queue
Agents surface content brief ideas every Monday — based on trending queries, competitor gaps, and your own performance patterns. You approve or reject. You stop being the strategist; you become the editor.
C
Content Genome
Every published asset is fingerprinted — argument structure, tone, evidence style. When a Salesforce post performs well, the system auto-suggests remix briefs for Dynamics, KPO, and EI.
D
Revision Intelligence
An orchestrator layer learns which revision patterns lead to Gate approval. After a few cycles, it pre-applies common corrections before content ever reaches a human reviewer.

Discussion Points
1  BU Progress
Is 62 assets per BU per quarter the right unit of measure — or should we track by impact, not volume?
Consider: a CMO may care more about pipeline influenced than asset count.
2  Activity Feed
Who actually reads a live agent feed? Does this belong on the home screen — or is it noise for most roles?
Alternative: only surface feed items that require action.
What's the FIRST thing the CMO needs to see when they log in?
1  Audience
Should the Marketing Head ever see this screen? Or is the Agent Floor only for Campaign Leads and AI COE?
Risk: showing agents to non-technical users may erode trust if something looks broken.
2  Gate Count
4 sequential gates — is that too many? Each gate is a potential bottleneck. What's the minimum number of human checkpoints we actually need?
Hypothesis: 2 gates (strategic validation + final review) may be enough.
What would make a user trust this system enough to reduce gates over time?
1  Quality Scores
Do reviewers actually use these scores to make decisions — or do they read the content and form their own opinion regardless?
If scores don't change reviewer behaviour, they're decoration.
2  Revision Notes
Freeform text sent back to an AI agent — is that reliable enough? Should we have structured revision categories instead?
e.g. "Tone", "Accuracy", "Brand", "CTA" as selectable flags + a notes field.
3  The Queue
14 assets in one sitting is a lot to ask. Should the system triage the queue — showing the highest-risk or highest-value assets first?
What would make reviewers want to use this every day?
→  Command Layer
Does natural language control feel like magic or like extra work? What's the right balance between guided UI and open-ended commands?
